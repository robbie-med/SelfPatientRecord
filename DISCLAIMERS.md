# HealthBinder — Disclaimers and Limitations

## What This Application Is

HealthBinder is a **personal health organization and education tool** designed to help patients organize their own medical records, understand health information, and prepare for clinical appointments.

## What This Application Is NOT

- **Not an Electronic Medical Record (EMR)** used by clinicians or health systems
- **Not a diagnostic device** and cannot diagnose any condition
- **Not a medical treatment system** and cannot prescribe, recommend, or modify treatments
- **Not a substitute for a licensed clinician**, physician, nurse, or pharmacist
- **Not represented as HIPAA-compliant** unless deployed under a proper HIPAA-compliant arrangement with appropriate safeguards
- **Not for use in emergencies** — call 911 or your local emergency number immediately

## Accuracy Limitations

- AI-extracted information may be incorrect, incomplete, or misinterpreted
- Medical records contain errors, copied-forward diagnoses, and outdated information
- The app may reflect those source errors
- Reference ranges and guidelines vary by laboratory, country, and population
- This app does not validate clinical information against an authoritative medical standard

## AI and Privacy

- AI features are **disabled by default**
- When AI features are enabled, selected text from your records may be transmitted to a remote AI service
- Encryption in transit reduces but does not eliminate all privacy, security, legal, or re-identification risks
- "Encrypted and processed through a privacy-preserving proxy" is not the same as "completely private"
- Review the privacy documentation of any AI provider you configure
- You control what you paste or upload

## Data Control

- Your health data is stored locally on your device or server
- You can export all your data at any time
- You can delete all your data at any time
- No data is sent to HealthBinder developers or any third party without your explicit action

## Liability

This software is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. The authors and contributors are not liable for any harm, loss, or consequences arising from the use of this application.

## Emergency Warning

**If you are experiencing a medical emergency, stop using this app and call 911 (US) or your local emergency number immediately.**

Signs of medical emergency include but are not limited to: chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, and pregnancy emergencies.

This app cannot triage emergencies.
